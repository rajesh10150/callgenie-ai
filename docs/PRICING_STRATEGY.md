# CallGenie AI — SaaS Pricing & Growth Strategy

## Pricing Model

### Free Plan — $0/month
- 30 AI call minutes
- 1 campaign
- 100 leads
- GPT-4.1 only
- English only
- Basic analytics
- Community support
- "Powered by CallGenie" watermark on calls

### Starter Plan — $49/month
- 500 AI call minutes ($0.12/min overage)
- 5 campaigns
- 2,500 leads
- GPT-4.1 + Gemini
- English + Hindi
- Standard analytics
- Email support
- CSV import
- Call recordings (30-day retention)

### Professional Plan — $149/month
- 2,000 AI call minutes ($0.10/min overage)
- 25 campaigns
- 25,000 leads
- All AI models
- All 5 languages
- Advanced analytics + reports
- Priority support
- WhatsApp automation
- API access
- Webhook integrations
- Call recordings (90-day retention)
- Team collaboration (up to 10 members)

### Enterprise Plan — $499+/month
- Unlimited AI call minutes
- Unlimited campaigns
- Unlimited leads
- All AI models + custom
- All languages + custom
- Custom analytics + BI integration
- Dedicated account manager
- Custom integrations
- SLA guarantee (99.9% uptime)
- White-labeling
- Dedicated infrastructure
- Unlimited team members
- Unlimited recording retention

## Cost Structure (Per Call)

| Component | Cost |
|-----------|------|
| Twilio (telephony) | ~$0.02/min |
| ElevenLabs (TTS) | ~$0.03/min |
| Deepgram (STT) | ~$0.01/min |
| AI model (avg) | ~$0.02/min |
| Infrastructure | ~$0.005/min |
| **Total COGS** | **~$0.085/min** |
| **Selling price** | **$0.10-0.15/min** |
| **Gross margin** | **15-43%** |

## SaaS Positioning

### Brand Identity
- **Tagline**: "Your AI Sales Team That Never Sleeps"
- **Position**: Premium AI-powered sales automation for growth-focused SMBs
- **Differentiator**: Only platform with Indian language support + multi-model AI

### Target Customer Profile
1. **Primary**: Real estate agencies (50-500 leads/month)
2. **Secondary**: Insurance brokers, education consultancies
3. **Tertiary**: Clinics, salons, loan companies
